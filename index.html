Biography

Melodic Techno Techno Peak Time Progressive

Lunr found his love for electronic music the same way most people do. On the dancefloor. As a raver first, he chased the feeling of a room completely lost in the music, everyone moving as one. That feeling never left him.

Today, based between Riyadh and Jeddah, his sets blend Melodic Techno and Techno Peak Time. But it goes beyond playing tracks. It is about creating a moment where the crowd forgets the world outside and just dances. His sound is dark and groove driven, built on high energy that pulls a room straight onto the floor and keeps it there.

For Lunr, music is more than performance. It is a craft he is constantly investing in. He is currently deepening his skills in production, because he believes real connection on the dancefloor starts with real intention behind the sound.

The underground scene is not just a genre or a venue for him. It is a community built on openness, love, and the pure joy of moving together.
